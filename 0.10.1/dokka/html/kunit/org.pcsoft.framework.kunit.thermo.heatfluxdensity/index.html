KHeatFluxDensityUnit

enum KHeatFluxDensityUnit : KUnit, Enum<KHeatFluxDensityUnit>

Enumerates concrete units of heat flux density - power per unit of area (a constructed quantity: mass · time⁻³). baseValue is the factor to convert into the group's base unit (BASE, watt per square meter): 1 unit = baseValue * W/m².

KHeatFluxDensityUnitInstance
Link copied to clipboard
class KHeatFluxDensityUnitInstance : KUnitInstance<KHeatFluxDensityUnitInstance> , KUnitMeasurable

Wraps a KMixedUnitInstance representing a heat flux density (power per unit of area), i.e. exactly two terms in the canonical normal form mass¹ · time⁻³ (kg·s⁻³ = W/m²). The value is always normalized internally to watts per square meter (KHeatFluxDensityUnit.BASE).
