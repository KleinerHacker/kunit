KMomentumUnit

enum KMomentumUnit : KUnit, Enum<KMomentumUnit>

Enumerates concrete units of momentum (a constructed quantity: mass · length · time⁻¹). baseValue is the factor to convert into the group's base unit (BASE, kilogram meter per second): 1 unit = baseValue * kg·m/s.

KMomentumUnitInstance
Link copied to clipboard
class KMomentumUnitInstance : KUnitInstance<KMomentumUnitInstance> , KUnitMeasurable

Wraps a KMixedUnitInstance representing a momentum (and equally an impulse), i.e. exactly three terms in the canonical normal form - KMassUnit.BASE (gram) at exponent +1, KDistanceUnit.BASE (meter) at exponent +1 and KTimeUnit.BASE (second) at exponent -1 (g·m·s⁻¹). The value is the raw component value; readings in kg·m/s (KMomentumUnit.BASE) divide by KGMS_IN_BASE.

fun KMixedUnitInstance.toMomentum(): KMomentumUnitInstance

Converts this mixed unit to a "pure" momentum, as long as it matches the canonical normal form: exactly one KMassUnit term at exponent +1, one KDistanceUnit term at exponent +1 and one KTimeUnit term at exponent -1 (order independent). The terms are normalized to their component base units.
