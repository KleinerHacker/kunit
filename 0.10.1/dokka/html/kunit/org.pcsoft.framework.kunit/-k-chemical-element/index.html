KChemicalElement

enum KChemicalElement : Enum<KChemicalElement>

The chemical elements of the classic school periodic table: the main and sub groups of periods 1-6 without the f-block (the lanthanides 57-71 are omitted, hence the gap between BARIUM and HAFNIUM; actinides and the transactinides are not covered at all).

Every entry carries the positional data of the periodic table (ordinalNumber, symbol, fullName, period, mainGroup/subGroup, category) plus its physical constants as typed unit instances of this library, so they compose directly with the rest of the framework:

val block = KChemicalElement.IRON.density!! * (2 of liters) // mass of 2 l of iron
KChemicalElement.IRON.molarMass into gramsPerMole            // 55.845

Constants that are not meaningfully defined for an element (e.g. the melting point of helium at normal pressure, or the resistivity of a noble gas) are null; every such property has a matching has... companion property (e.g. hasDensity) to test for availability without null handling.
