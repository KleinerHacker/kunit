KVolumetricHeatCapacityUnit

enum KVolumetricHeatCapacityUnit : KUnit, Enum<KVolumetricHeatCapacityUnit>

Enumerates concrete units of volumetric heat capacity (heat capacity per volume; a constructed quantity: mass · length⁻¹ · time⁻² · temperature⁻¹). baseValue is the factor to convert into the group's base unit (BASE, joule per cubic meter kelvin): 1 unit = baseValue * J/(m³·K).

KVolumetricHeatCapacityUnitInstance
Link copied to clipboard
class KVolumetricHeatCapacityUnitInstance : KUnitInstance<KVolumetricHeatCapacityUnitInstance> , KUnitMeasurable

Wraps a KMixedUnitInstance representing a volumetric heat capacity (heat capacity per volume), i.e. exactly four terms in the canonical normal form - KMassUnit.BASE (gram) at exponent +1, KDistanceUnit.BASE (meter) at exponent -1, KTimeUnit.BASE (second) at exponent -2 and KTemperatureDifferenceUnit.BASE at exponent -1 (kg·m⁻¹·s⁻²·K⁻¹ = J/(m³·K)).

toVolumetricHeatCapacity

fun KMixedUnitInstance.toVolumetricHeatCapacity(): KVolumetricHeatCapacityUnitInstance

Converts this mixed unit to a "pure" volumetric heat capacity, as long as it matches the canonical normal form: exactly one KMassUnit term at exponent +1, one KDistanceUnit term at exponent -1, one KTimeUnit term at exponent -2 and one KTemperatureDifferenceUnit term at exponent -1 (order independent). The terms are normalized over their org.pcsoft.framework.kunit.KUnit.baseValues and bridged from the gram-based product to the kilogram-based joule.
