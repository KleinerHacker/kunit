KAmountOfSubstanceUnit

enum KAmountOfSubstanceUnit : KUnit, Enum<KAmountOfSubstanceUnit>

Enumerates concrete units of amount of substance (a native, measurable SI base quantity). baseValue is the factor to convert into the group's base unit (BASE, mole): 1 unit = baseValue * mol.

KAmountOfSubstanceUnitInstance

class KAmountOfSubstanceUnitInstance : KUnitMeasurable, KUnitInstance<KAmountOfSubstanceUnitInstance>

An amount of substance value: the "pure" wrapper of the amount-of-substance group, a single KAmountOfSubstanceUnit.BASE (mole) term at exponent 1, always normalized internally to moles.
