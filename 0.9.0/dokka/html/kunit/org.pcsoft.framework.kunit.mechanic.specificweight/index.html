enum KSpecificWeightUnit : KUnit, Enum<KSpecificWeightUnit>

Enumerates concrete units of specific weight (weight force per volume; a constructed quantity: mass · length⁻² · time⁻²). baseValue is the factor to convert into the group's base unit (BASE, newton per cubic meter): 1 unit = baseValue * N/m³.

KSpecificWeightUnitInstance
Link copied to clipboard
class KSpecificWeightUnitInstance : KUnitInstance<KSpecificWeightUnitInstance> , KUnitMeasurable

Wraps a KMixedUnitInstance representing a specific weight (weight force per volume), i.e. exactly three terms - KMassUnit.BASE (gram) at exponent +1, KDistanceUnit.BASE (meter) at exponent -2 and KTimeUnit.BASE (second) at exponent -2 (g·m⁻²·s⁻²). The value is the raw component value; readings in newtons per cubic meter (KSpecificWeightUnit.BASE) divide by N_PER_M3_IN_BASE - the same convention the neighbouring force, pressure and density groups use.

fun KMixedUnitInstance.toSpecificWeight(): KSpecificWeightUnitInstance

Converts this mixed unit to a "pure" specific weight, as long as it matches the canonical specific weight normal form: exactly one KMassUnit term at exponent +1, one KDistanceUnit term at exponent -2 and one KTimeUnit term at exponent -2 (order independent). The terms are normalized over their org.pcsoft.framework.kunit.KUnit.baseValues.
