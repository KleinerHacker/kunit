KMassFlowUnit

enum KMassFlowUnit : KUnit, Enum<KMassFlowUnit>

Enumerates concrete units of mass flow rate (a constructed quantity: mass · time⁻¹). baseValue is the factor to convert into the group's base unit (BASE, kilogram per second): 1 unit = baseValue * kg/s.

KMassFlowUnitInstance
Link copied to clipboard
class KMassFlowUnitInstance : KUnitInstance<KMassFlowUnitInstance> , KUnitMeasurable

Wraps a KMixedUnitInstance representing a mass flow rate, i.e. exactly two terms in the canonical normal form - KMassUnit.BASE (gram) at exponent +1 and KTimeUnit.BASE (second) at exponent -1 (g·s⁻¹). The value is the raw component value; readings in kg/s (KMassFlowUnit.BASE) divide by KGS_IN_BASE.
