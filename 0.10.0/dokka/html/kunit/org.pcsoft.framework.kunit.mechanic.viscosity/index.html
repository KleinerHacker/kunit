KViscosityUnit

enum KViscosityUnit : KUnit, Enum<KViscosityUnit>

Enumerates concrete units of dynamic viscosity (a constructed quantity: mass · length⁻¹ · time⁻¹, i.e. pressure · time). baseValue is the factor to convert into the group's base unit (BASE, pascal second): 1 unit = baseValue * Pa·s.

KViscosityUnitInstance
Link copied to clipboard
class KViscosityUnitInstance : KUnitInstance<KViscosityUnitInstance> , KUnitMeasurable

Wraps a KMixedUnitInstance representing a dynamic viscosity, i.e. exactly three terms in the canonical normal form - KMassUnit.BASE (gram) at +1, KDistanceUnit.BASE (meter) at -1 and KTimeUnit.BASE (second) at -1 (g·m⁻¹·s⁻¹). The value is the raw component value; readings in Pa·s (KViscosityUnit.BASE) divide by PAS_IN_BASE.
