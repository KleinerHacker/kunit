KDiffusivityUnit

enum KDiffusivityUnit : KUnit, Enum<KDiffusivityUnit>

Enumerates concrete units of diffusivity (a constructed quantity: distance² · time⁻¹). baseValue is the factor to convert into the group's base unit (BASE, square meter per second): 1 unit = baseValue * m²/s.

The group lives in common because the same quantity belongs to two subject areas: it is the thermal diffusivity α = λ / (ρ · c_p) of thermodynamics and the kinematic viscosity ν = η / ρ of mechanics. Both readings share this vocabulary; the stokes (STOKES, CENTISTOKES) is the traditional kinematic-viscosity spelling.

Example:

KDiffusivityUnit.SQUARE_MILLIMETER_PER_SECOND.baseValue // 1e-6 (the unit material tables use)

SQUARE_MILLIMETER_PER_SECOND

Square millimeter per second ("mm²/s"), 1 mm²/s = 1e-6 m²/s - the magnitude material tables use (copper ≈ 111 mm²/s, water ≈ 0.14 mm²/s). Numerically identical to the centistokes.
