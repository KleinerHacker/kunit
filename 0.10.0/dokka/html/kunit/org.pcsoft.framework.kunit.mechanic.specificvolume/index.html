enum KSpecificVolumeUnit : KUnit, Enum<KSpecificVolumeUnit>

Enumerates concrete units of specific volume (volume per mass, the reciprocal of density; a constructed quantity: length³ · mass⁻¹). baseValue is the factor to convert into the group's base unit (BASE, cubic meter per kilogram): 1 unit = baseValue * m³/kg.

KSpecificVolumeUnitInstance
Link copied to clipboard
class KSpecificVolumeUnitInstance : KUnitInstance<KSpecificVolumeUnitInstance> , KUnitMeasurable

Wraps a KMixedUnitInstance representing a specific volume, i.e. exactly two terms in the canonical normal form - KDistanceUnit.BASE (meter) at exponent +3 and KMassUnit.BASE (gram) at exponent -1 (m³·g⁻¹). The value is the raw component value; readings in m³/kg (KSpecificVolumeUnit.BASE) divide by M3KG_IN_BASE.
