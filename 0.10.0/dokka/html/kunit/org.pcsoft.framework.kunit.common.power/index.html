KPowerUnit

enum KPowerUnit : KUnit, Enum<KPowerUnit>

Enumerates concrete units of power (a constructed quantity: mass¹ · distance² · time⁻³, i.e. kg·m²·s⁻³). baseValue is the factor to convert into the group's base unit (BASE, watt): 1 unit = baseValue * W.

KPowerUnitInstance
Link copied to clipboard
class KPowerUnitInstance : KUnitInstance<KPowerUnitInstance> , KUnitMeasurable

Wraps a KMixedUnitInstance representing a power, i.e. exactly three terms in the canonical normal form mass¹ · distance² · time⁻³ (kg·m²·s⁻³). The value is always normalized internally to watts (KPowerUnit.BASE), regardless of which KPowerUnit, SI KUnitPrefix, or mass/length/time combination it was constructed from.

toPower
Link copied to clipboard
fun KMixedUnitInstance.toPower(): KPowerUnitInstance

Converts this mixed unit to a "pure" power value, as long as it matches the canonical power normal form: exactly one KMassUnit term at exponent +1, one KDistanceUnit term at exponent +2 and one KTimeUnit term at exponent -3 (order independent). The terms are normalized over their org.pcsoft.framework.kunit.KUnit.baseValues, so the resulting power is expressed in watts regardless of which concrete mass/length/time units the terms were tagged with.
