KLineForceUnit

enum KLineForceUnit : KUnit, Enum<KLineForceUnit>

Enumerates concrete units of force per length (a constructed quantity: mass · time⁻²). baseValue is the factor to convert into the group's base unit (BASE, newton per meter): 1 unit = baseValue * N/m.

KLineForceUnitInstance
Link copied to clipboard
class KLineForceUnitInstance : KUnitInstance<KLineForceUnitInstance> , KUnitMeasurable

Wraps a KMixedUnitInstance representing a force per length - read either as a surface tension or as a stiffness / spring rate - i.e. exactly two terms in the canonical normal form: KMassUnit.BASE (gram) at exponent +1 and KTimeUnit.BASE (second) at exponent -2 (g·s⁻²). The value is the raw component value; readings in N/m (KLineForceUnit.BASE) divide by NM_IN_BASE.
